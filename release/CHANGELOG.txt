Cinematic Combat (CombatMod) - changelog
========================================

0.9.0-beta  (first public release)
  * Open source under the MIT license: https://github.com/QuickkNastyy/CrimsonDesert-CinematicCombat
  * Slow-motion amplifier for the game's own slow-motion moments, with strength,
    ramp-in and ramp-out sliders and a chain safety limit.
  * Launch (knock-back) boost with strength, minimum launch and angle settings;
    explosive arrows and bombs are never boosted.
  * Hit-stop control (experimental).
  * Blinding Flash modes: the game's own, fast chain + slow finale, smooth slow
    motion per hit + slow finale.
  * Slow motion for towers / explosive arrows, and for explosions you cause.
  * Custom slow-motion triggers (ini rules).
  * In-game control panel (Ctrl+Enter or LB + DPad-Down): pauses the game,
    presets (Vanilla, Cinematic, Heavy hits, Extreme), Save & Close, side help
    window, keyboard / mouse / controller control.
  * Finds the game's functions by byte signatures (CombatMod.sigs) and checks the
    game's data layout; it stays inert, instead of risking a crash, on a game
    build it does not recognise.
  * Supported game builds (Steam build ID): 26308950, 26317560, 26329202.
  * Hit reactions: the finale of a Blinding Flash chain can be a knock-down,
    ground throw or ragdoll fling (default: ground throw), and Turning Slash
    hits can be given the same choice (default: the game's own). Applied to
    your own hits only, one call at a time, never stored.
  * Blinding Flash chain speed is capped at 1.0 (higher speeds would speed up
    everyone); an [Advanced] switch lifts the cap for experiments.
  * "Longest chain" is now called "Slow-motion cap per chain" and says what it
    does: it only shortens slow motion.
  * Safety: before the panel pauses the game or a custom trigger changes the
    clock, the mod checks that the game's clock objects look right and refuses
    to write otherwise (it logs why). A missing or unreadable CombatMod.ini
    never turns on any developer feature.
  * The first lines of CombatMod.log name the mod version and the game build.
    If a needed function is not found at start-up, the search is repeated
    once after about 10 seconds.
  * The Cinematic, Heavy hits and Extreme presets keep your Minimum launch.
  * Release defaults: slow motion x0.5 with 1.6x ramps, launch x2 with minimum
    launch 25, tower slow motion on, Blinding Flash and hit-stop untouched,
    Blinding Flash finale as a ground throw, developer options (local network
    port, telemetry) off.
